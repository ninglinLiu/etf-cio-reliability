规则与成交时序

after close[t]:
  if fast[t-1] <= slow[t-1] and fast[t] > slow[t]: BUY
  if fast[t-1] >= slow[t-1] and fast[t] < slow[t]: SELL
  BUY quantity = floor((cash[t] * allocation - fixed_fee) / conservative_close_cost[t])
  freeze quantity; submit Backtrader Market order
at open[t+1]:
  BUY: execute the already-fixed quantity, or reject for insufficient cash; NEVER resize
  SELL: liquidate held shares; include entry/exit costs and dividends in PnL
at close[t+1]: credit prior-holder dividend; equity = broker cash + broker shares × raw close
